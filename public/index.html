⇌ BRIDGE
Understand, don't win.

Talk to someone who disagrees.

BRIDGE pairs you, anonymously, with a stranger who holds the opposite view on a charged topic. You both share your reasoning up front, and a mediator keeps things human. The goal isn't to win — it's to understand why a reasonable person could see it differently.

  • 🕶️ Fully anonymous — nickname only, no accounts, no IP logging.
  • 🤝 Assume good faith. Attack ideas, not people.
  • ❓ Ask at least one genuine question.
  • 🚪 Leave or report anytime.

Conversations are ephemeral and never stored. See how privacy works in the README.
